SpartanGO UX/UI User Personas

Overview

These personas represent two potential users of SpartanGO, a campus shuttle application designed for Norfolk State University students. They help guide the design of an easy-to-use campus transportation experience.

Persona 1: Jasmine Carter

Age: 20
Role: Full-time NSU student
Primary Need: Track campus shuttles and arrive at class on time.

User Quote

“I need to know when my shuttle is coming so I can get to class on time.”

Goals

* View live shuttle locations.
* Check estimated arrival times.
* Find approved pickup stops.
* Receive notifications about delays.
* Request rides through the mobile app.

Pain Points

* Uncertainty about shuttle arrival times.
* Worry about arriving late to class.
* Difficulty planning trips when shuttle information is unclear.
* Having to repeatedly check for updates.

Technology Behavior

* Uses a smartphone throughout the school day.
* Prefers simple navigation and readable information.
* Wants important shuttle updates to be easy to find.

Design Considerations

* Display shuttle locations and arrival estimates on the home screen.
* Make pickup stops easy to search and select.
* Provide clear notifications about meaningful changes.
* Keep the ride-request process simple.

Persona 2: Malik Johnson

Age: 24
Role: NSU student and part-time worker
Primary Need: Coordinate shuttle trips around classes and work.

User Quote

“I plan my day around the shuttle, so missed connections affect my whole schedule.”

Goals

* Identify the best shuttle and pickup stop.
* Check arrival times before leaving.
* Receive service updates.
* Access a digital QR pass for boarding.
* Receive clear confirmation when a ride request succeeds.

Pain Points

* Limited flexibility when transportation is delayed.
* Uncertainty about route changes.
* Difficulty planning around unreliable shuttle information.
* Needing to find a boarding pass quickly.

Technology Behavior

* Uses mobile apps to organize daily activities.
* Prefers straightforward navigation and quick feedback.
* Values convenient access to transportation information.

Design Considerations

* Present routes, arrival estimates, and shuttle status clearly.
* Make the QR pass accessible from the main navigation.
* Communicate successful and unsuccessful actions.
* Display service disruptions clearly.

⸻

Shared User Needs

Both personas benefit from:

* Clear shuttle locations and estimated arrival times.
* Easy pickup-stop selection.
* Timely notifications.
* Simple ride-request workflows.
* Convenient QR boarding.
* Readable mobile screens.
* Clear feedback when a service is unavailable.

Design Objective

SpartanGO aims to make campus transportation easier to navigate by providing students with clear shuttle information, convenient ride requests, and a secure digital boarding experience.
